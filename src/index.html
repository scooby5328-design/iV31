How the IV calculator works

The basics

Every stat since Ruby comes from the species' base stat, the Pokémon's IV, its EVs (points earned from battles and vitamins), its level and its nature. The calculator tries every IV from 0 to 31 and keeps the ones that match your numbers.

The formula

HP = ⌊(2×Base + IV + ⌊EV/4⌋) × Level/100⌋ + Level + 10
Other = ⌊(⌊(2×Base + IV + ⌊EV/4⌋) × Level/100⌋ + 5) × Nature⌋
Nature is 1.1 for the raised stat, 0.9 for the lowered one, otherwise 1.

Key stats and natures

Starred stats (★) are the two this Pokémon relies on most, judged from its final evolution in the selected generation. The suggested natures raise one of them and lower the attacking stat it doesn't use. HP is never starred because no nature changes it.

EV calculator

Type the EVs your Pokémon has, or add them as you train: pick what you defeated, how many times, and any Macho Brace, Power item or Pokérus. It keeps you inside the limits (510 in total, and 252 per stat from Gen VI on, 255 before that) and shows the stat points the EVs give at its level and at level 100. Use "Send EVs to Summary" so the IV readout accounts for them.

Hidden Power

Hidden Power's type comes from whether each IV is odd or even, and in Gen III to V its power (30 to 70) comes from the second-lowest bit of each IV. The "Aim for a type" table lists the highest IVs that give each type, which is handy when breeding.

Updates

When iv31 starts it quietly checks GitHub for a newer version. If there is one, a bar appears at the top: choose Update now and it downloads the update, checks its fingerprint, installs it and restarts, keeping everything you've saved. You can also click the version number to check any time. Nothing about you is sent; it only downloads a small version file.

Pokémon that have battled

The stats screen only shows each IV and a quarter of its EVs added together, so iv31 can't always tell them apart. Set EVs on a level entry to None yet for a Pokémon that's just been caught or hatched, Has battled if it has fought and you don't know its EVs, or leave it on Not sure to let iv31 decide stat by stat. When EVs are raising a stat, the readout shows the IV and EV ranges that fit. Typing known EVs, or adding the stats again at a higher level, narrows them. To see what one training session added, use EVs gained below the EV calculator.

Gen I and II

Red, Blue, Yellow, Gold, Silver and Crystal use DVs from 0 to 15 instead of IVs, Stat Experience instead of EVs, and no natures. The HP DV isn't stored; it comes from whether the other four DVs are odd or even, so iv31 only keeps DV combinations where they agree. Gen I has one Special stat. In Gen II, Sp. Atk and Sp. Def share the Special DV, and DVs also decide Hidden Power and whether a Pokémon is shiny.

Getting accurate results

Ranges are widest at low levels. A freshly caught Pokémon has 0 EVs, so check it before it battles. Changed base stats from older games show in red. Let's Go Pikachu/Eevee and Legends: Arceus use different stat systems and aren't supported.
