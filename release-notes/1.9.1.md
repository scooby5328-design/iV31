## What's new
- **Much faster Poké Data:** the table opens in a fraction of the time (up to 30 times faster for the newer games with over 1,000 Pokémon). Rows appear as you scroll down instead of all at once.
- **Faster start-up:** iv31 shows its first screen about three times sooner.
- **Faster tab switching and game changes.**

## Install
**Online:** open **scooby5328-design.github.io/iV31** (it updates by itself).

**Windows, Mac or the Linux .tar.gz on 1.2.0 or newer:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`

**AppImage:** download the new AppImage below.
