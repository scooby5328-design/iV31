## What's new
- **Best routes to train it:** every Pokémon's data page now lists the best routes to train its two key stats in the game you've picked, under **Training focus**. Each route shows how many of the Pokémon you'll meet there give the right EVs, which Pokémon they are (tap one to open its page), what each gives and how it's found, such as fishing or surfing. The top 5 are shown, with the rest a tap away.
- Routes rank higher when more of the Pokémon there are useful. Gifts, one-time and roaming Pokémon aren't counted, since you can't battle them again and again.
- The ★ priority routes in Poké Data use the same rules.

## Install
**Online:** open **scooby5328-design.github.io/iV31** (it updates by itself).

**Windows:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now** and it installs through the installer.

**Mac or the Linux .tar.gz:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`

**AppImage:** download the new AppImage below.
