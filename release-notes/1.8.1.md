## What's new
- **EV Training is now Poké Data:** one table of every Pokémon's fixed data, in Pokédex order.
  - New **Type** and **Weak to** columns (a red outline means 4×).
  - **Matchup against:** pick an attacking type to see how hard it hits every Pokémon, then show only those weak to it, resisting it or immune.
  - **Type** filter, and search now matches types too (type "fire").
  - Click a type chip in a row to filter by that type, or a weakness chip to see everything weak to it.
  - The **Matchup checker** for any one or two types sits at the top, with a button to filter the table to that type.
- **Quality of life:**
  - Your filters, sorting and column choices are remembered.
  - **Clear filters** resets everything in one click, and the count line says which filters are on.
  - Press **/** to jump to the search box and **Esc** to clear it.
  - Hide the **Weak to** or **Found in** columns for a narrower table.
  - The table shows its first rows right away and fills in the rest, so the tab opens faster.
- **EVs gained** and **EV-lowering berries** moved to the IV Calculator tab, under the EV calculator, so the training tools are together.
- The **Types** tab keeps the weakness chart and the notes for your game.

## Install
**Online:** open **scooby5328-design.github.io/iV31** (it updates by itself).

**Windows, Mac or the Linux .tar.gz on 1.2.0 or newer:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`

**AppImage:** download the new AppImage below.
