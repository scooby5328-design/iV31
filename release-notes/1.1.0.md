## What's new
- **EV calculator** next to the IV readout. Track EVs per stat, add them by Pokémon defeated (with Macho Brace, Power items and Pokérus) or by vitamins, and see the stat points they give at your level and at level 100.
- **Hidden Power calculator** with the type, power and Gen III physical/special category for any six IVs, plus a table of the best IVs for every type.
- **Secret Power tab** listing every area in Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, FireRed and LeafGreen with the move Secret Power turns into there, worked out from the games' map data. Gen IV to VII show the terrain table.
- The "EVs" tab is now called "EV yield".

## Install
Download **iv31-Setup-1.1.0.exe.zip** below, right-click it, choose **Extract All**, then run the installer. It installs over older versions and keeps your saved data.

This is an old version. The latest release updates itself, so new users should download that one instead.
