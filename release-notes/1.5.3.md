## What's new
- **More accurate story order.** Every game's order for sorting by **Found in** was checked against Bulbapedia's walkthroughs and corrected. For example:
  - **Red, Blue, Yellow:** Routes 16–18, Fuchsia City and the Safari Zone now come before Routes 12–15.
  - **Gold, Silver, Crystal, HeartGold, SoulSilver:** Kanto follows the post-League route from Vermilion City, and in HeartGold and SoulSilver Routes 47 and 48 and the Johto Safari Zone move to the post-game.
  - **Diamond, Pearl, Platinum:** Route 218, Canalave City and Iron Island come before Lake Valor; Routes 219–221, Fuego Ironworks and Old Chateau move to the post-game.
  - **Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald:** Routes 105–109 come right after Rustboro, and Sky Pillar sits after the Cave of Origin.
  - **Black, White:** Routes 17 and 18 and the P2 Laboratory come right after Celestial Tower.
  - **X, Y:** Route 22 moves to just before Victory Road, and Terminus Cave to the post-game.
  - **Sun, Moon, Ultra Sun, Ultra Moon:** Haina Desert, Ruins of Abundance and Melemele Sea move to the post-game.
  - **Sword, Shield:** the northern Wild Area now comes after Motostoke Outskirts, and the Isle of Armor after Motostoke.
