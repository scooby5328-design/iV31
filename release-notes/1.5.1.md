## What's new
- **Sort the EV table by route.** Click the **Found in** heading in EV Training to list Pokémon in route order: everything on Route 101 first, then Route 102, and so on, followed by other areas such as caves and towns from A to Z. Click again to reverse it. Pokémon you can't find in the wild always stay at the bottom.
- **Fixed names:** Farfetch'd, Sirfetch'd and Flabébé showed odd characters. Searches now also find them if you type a plain apostrophe or no accent ("farfetch'd", "flabebe").

## Install
**Windows, Mac or the Linux .tar.gz on 1.2.0 or newer:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`
