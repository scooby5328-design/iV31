## What's new
- **iv31 is now a handheld device.** The whole app sits inside a graphite field unit with a blue main screen, a small status screen for the game, version and clock, and real buttons:
  - **D-pad:** ◂ ▸ switch tabs (on a Pokémon's data page they step through the Pokédex), ▴ ▾ scroll the screen.
  - **B** goes back and **A** goes forward through the pages you've visited.
  - **SOUND** turns the button clicks on or off, and **DAY/NIGHT** switches between a pale day screen and a glowing night screen.
- **Device feel:** a start-up screen when iv31 opens, a screen flicker when you change pages, button press animations and soft click sounds.
- Everything on the screen keeps the pixel text, now on blue screens.
- On phones the device frame is slimmer and the Back and Forward buttons sit at the top of the screen.

## Install
**Windows, Mac or the Linux .tar.gz on 1.2.0 or newer:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`

**AppImage:** download the new AppImage below.
