## What's new
- **IVs for Pokémon that have already battled.** Each level entry in the IV calculator now has an **EVs** choice:
  - **Not sure** (the default): stats that fit with no EVs are read as having none; stats that are too high are read as raised by EVs.
  - **None yet**: just caught or hatched.
  - **Has battled**: EVs unknown in every stat.
  When EVs are raising a stat, the readout shows the IV range and the EV range that fit, using the limits of 31 per IV, 255 per stat (252 from Gen VI) and 510 in total, instead of "No match". Typing the EVs you know under **Known EVs** narrows it. Gen I and II get the same for Stat Exp.
- **EVs gained** in the EV Training tab. Type a Pokémon's stats before you train and again after, and iv31 shows how many EVs the training added in each stat, how many it has now, and how much room is left. **Add to EV calculator** sends them on. It remembers what you typed, so you can close iv31 while you train.

## Install
**Windows, Mac or the Linux .tar.gz on 1.2.0 or newer:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.
