## What's new
- **One game picker.** Choose your generation and version once in the top bar. Every tab follows it: base stats, types, locations, berries and Secret Power.
- **Types tab.** A weakness and resistance chart for the selected generation, with or without the Fairy type, and Gen III physical/special marks. Pick a Pokémon or two types to see its 4× and 2× weaknesses, resistances and immunities.
- **EV-lowering berries.** A table of the six berries that lower EVs, the vitamin that raises each stat, how they work in your game, and where to get them. In Ruby, Sapphire, FireRed and LeafGreen they can't be used on Pokémon; the app tells you.
- **Wild locations.** The EV yield table now has a **Found in** column for the selected version, search by place (for example "Route 110"), and a filter for Pokémon you can find in that version.
- **Favorites.** Tap ☆ next to any Pokémon to save it. The Favorites tab shows everything about it on one card: base stats, key stats, natures, weaknesses, EV yield, evolution line and locations.
- **Types in the IV readout.** It now shows the Pokémon's types and what it's weak to.
- **Cleaner layout.** Five tabs: IV Calculator (with the EV and Hidden Power calculators), EV Training, Types, Secret Power and Favorites. The IV calculator starts empty.
- **Source code.** The app's source and build script are now in the repository.

## Install
**Already have 1.2.0?** iv31 offers this update by itself the next time it starts. Click **Update now**.

**New, or on 1.1.0 or older?** Download **iv31-Setup-1.3.0.exe.zip** below, right-click it, choose **Extract All**, then run the installer. It installs over older versions and keeps your saved data. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.
