## What's new
- **Every section folds.** Each section's title bar now has the same **Close ▴ / Open ▾** button as the matchup checker; click the button or anywhere on the bar. iv31 remembers which sections you closed.
- **SIZE control:** − and + on the device's left panel make everything on the screen bigger or smaller, from 80% to 150%. Ctrl and + / − (⌘ on a Mac) and Ctrl 0 work too. Your size is remembered.
- **D-pad ▴ ▾** now scroll half a screen at a time.

## Install
**Online:** open **scooby5328-design.github.io/iV31** (it updates by itself).

**Windows, Mac or the Linux .tar.gz on 1.2.0 or newer:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`

**AppImage:** download the new AppImage below.
