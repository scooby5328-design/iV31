## What's new
- **Priority routes in Poké Data:** pick a Pokémon in the IV Calculator and Poké Data marks the best places to train its two key stats. Under **Found in**, routes where 2 or more Pokémon give EVs in those stats are highlighted with a ★ and listed first. Point at one to see which Pokémon are there and what they give. A note above the table says which Pokémon it's set up for.
- **Closed sections are header-sized:** when you close the Matchup checker or Poké Data, it shrinks to just its title bar instead of leaving a big empty dotted box. Favorites no longer shows a large empty panel either.

## Install
**Online:** open **scooby5328-design.github.io/iV31** (it updates by itself).

**Windows:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now** and it installs through the installer.

**Mac or the Linux .tar.gz:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`

**AppImage:** download the new AppImage below.
