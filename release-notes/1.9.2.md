## What's new
- **Scales with the window:** make iv31 bigger or go full screen and the whole device grows to fill it: the shell, buttons, text and everything on the screen. Smaller windows and phones look the same as before. The **SIZE** buttons still make the screen text bigger or smaller on top of this.

## Install
**Online:** open **scooby5328-design.github.io/iV31** (it updates by itself).

**Windows, Mac or the Linux .tar.gz on 1.2.0 or newer:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`

**AppImage:** download the new AppImage below.
