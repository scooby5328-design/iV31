## Fixed
- **Mac: iv31 could stop opening after an update** and show the Neutralino website instead. Updates are now saved to a separate file first, checked, and only then swapped in, so a failed or interrupted update can't leave iv31 broken.
- **Restarting after an update** now works on Mac and Linux. Before, iv31 could sit on "Restarting…" until you closed it.

## If iv31 on your Mac shows the Neutralino website
Download **iv31-1.4.4-mac.zip** below, double-click it, and drag the new **iv31.app** into Applications, replacing the old one. Your favorites and settings are kept. Then run this once in **Terminal**:
```
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/iv31.app
```

## Install
**Windows, Mac or Linux .tar.gz on 1.2.0 or newer:** iv31 offers this update by itself.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`

**AppImage:** download the new AppImage below.
