## What's new
- **The Secret Power tab is now Powers**, with three charts of its own, picked with the tabs at the top:
  - **Hidden Power:** the highest IVs that give each type and the power they make. In Gold, Silver and Crystal it shows the type for every Attack and Defense DV. **Load** sends a row to the Hidden Power calculator.
  - **Secret Power:** what the move looks like and its extra effect in every area.
  - **Nature Power:** which attack it becomes in every area. Sword, Shield, Scarlet and Violet get a terrain list.

**Windows, Mac or the Linux .tar.gz on 1.2.0 or newer:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.
