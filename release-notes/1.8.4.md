## What's new
- **Folding panels are easier to spot.** The **Matchup checker**, **EVs gained**, **EV-lowering berries** and **How the IV calculator works** panels now have:
  - a dashed outline while folded
  - an arrow that turns when they open
  - a bright **Open ▾** button (it becomes **Close ▴** when open)
  - a highlight when you hover over them

## Install
**Online:** open **scooby5328-design.github.io/iV31** (it updates by itself).

**Windows, Mac or the Linux .tar.gz on 1.2.0 or newer:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`

**AppImage:** download the new AppImage below.
