## What's new
- **New device layout.** The controls now sit on wide panels on the left and right of the screen, and the screen runs the full height of the device.
  - **Left panel:** the game picker, the D-pad and the SOUND key.
  - **Right panel:** the version and clock, the B and A buttons, and the DAY/NIGHT key.
- On phones nothing changes: the game picker and version sit at the top and the screen fills the rest.

## Install
**Windows, Mac or the Linux .tar.gz on 1.2.0 or newer:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`

**AppImage:** download the new AppImage below.
