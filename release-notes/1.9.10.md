## What's new
- **Training routes skip places you can't battle in:** the Safari Zone in Red, Blue, Yellow, Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, FireRed and LeafGreen, the Great Marsh in Diamond, Pearl and Platinum, and the Johto Safari Zone in HeartGold and SoulSilver only let you throw balls and bait, so they give no EVs. They no longer appear as ★ priority routes in Poké Data or in the best routes on data pages. They still show under Found in as places to catch Pokémon.
- The Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire Safari Zone and X and Y's Friend Safari have normal battles, so they still count.

## Install
**Online:** open **scooby5328-design.github.io/iV31** (it updates by itself).

**Windows:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now** and it installs through the installer.

**Mac or the Linux .tar.gz:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`

**AppImage:** download the new AppImage below.
