## What's new
- **Sliding tabs:** on a computer the side tabs now tuck in to small symbols and slide out to show their names when you point at them.
- **Menu button:** the button with three lines above the tabs keeps them open, or tucks them away again. iv31 remembers your choice.
- **Tab symbols:** each tab has its own symbol: a calculator for IV Calculator, a table for Poké Data, three linked circles for Types, a lightning bolt for Powers and a star for Favorites. Phones show the symbols above the tab names too.

## Install
**Online:** open **scooby5328-design.github.io/iV31** (it updates by itself).

**Windows, Mac or the Linux .tar.gz on 1.2.0 or newer:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`

**AppImage:** download the new AppImage below.
