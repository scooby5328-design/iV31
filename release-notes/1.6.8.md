## What's new
- **A new look.** iv31 is redesigned as a retro field-guide screen: green-tinted screens with scanlines, pixel-style headings, glowing number readouts and chunky device buttons. It follows your system's light or dark setting (a pale LCD by day, a glowing green screen by night).
- **A data page for every Pokémon.** Click any Pokémon's name or number, in the EV Training table, the IV readout, Party comparison, Favorites, the matchup checker or an evolution line, to open its page: base stats, the 2 stats to focus, the proper nature, the EVs it gives, where to find it, its evolutions and its type matchups. Buttons there check its IVs, add it to your party, or add its EVs to the EV calculator.
- **Back and Forward** buttons at the top left move through the pages you've visited, like a browser (also Alt+← / Alt+→ and mouse side buttons). On Android, the back gesture steps back through pages before closing the app.
- **Strengths in the matchup checker:** besides what hurts a Pokémon, it now shows what its own types hit super effectively.
- **Type chart highlighting:** click as many types as you like to highlight their rows and columns.

## Install
**Windows, Mac or the Linux .tar.gz on 1.2.0 or newer:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`
