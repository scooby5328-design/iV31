## What's new
- **No more sideways scrolling on the screen.** Wide tables (EV Training, the type chart, the Secret Power and Nature Power charts, and the calculator tables) now shrink to fit the screen instead of showing a side-to-side scrollbar. Column headings wrap onto two lines where needed.

## Install
**Windows, Mac or the Linux .tar.gz on 1.2.0 or newer:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`

**AppImage:** download the new AppImage below.
