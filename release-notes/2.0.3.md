## What's new
- **How to evolve:** every Pokémon data page now lists what each evolution needs in the game you picked, under the Evolution line. For example Eevee shows all its stones, friendship by day or night, and the Moss Rock and Ice Rock spots.
- **Alternate branches:** regional evolutions like Perrserker, Sirfetch'd, Obstagoon and Clodsire now appear in the evolution line, marked with which form they need.
- **Game-aware:** if an evolution can't happen in your game (Leafeon in HeartGold, Espeon in FireRed), it says so and shows where it can.

## Install
**Online:** open **scooby5328-design.github.io/iV31** (it updates by itself).

**Windows:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now** and it installs through the installer.

**Mac or the Linux .tar.gz:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`
