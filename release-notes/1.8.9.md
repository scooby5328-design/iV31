## What's new
- **Tucked-in tabs stay put:** when the side tabs are tucked in, pointing at them or clicking one no longer slides the full tabs out over the page. The open tab simply lights up its square and symbol.
- **Full tabs only with the menu button:** the tab names show only after you click the three-line button to open them.

## Install
**Online:** open **scooby5328-design.github.io/iV31** (it updates by itself).

**Windows, Mac or the Linux .tar.gz on 1.2.0 or newer:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`

**AppImage:** download the new AppImage below.
