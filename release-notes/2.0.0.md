## What's new
- **Routes tab:** a new tab lists every route in the game you've picked. Numbered routes come first in order, then **Special areas** (caves, mountains, forests and the like, such as Mt. Pyre and Victory Road) and **Towns and cities**. Each place shows how many Pokémon live there, the best EVs per battle, EXP, items and the field moves it needs.
- **Sort for training:** sort every place by the EVs one battle gives on average for any stat, or by EXP. It counts how often each Pokémon appears, at the best spot on each route.
- **Route pages:** tap any place for its full page:
  - every wild Pokémon by area and by how you meet it, with levels, appearance rate as a percentage and time of day
  - hidden and special Pokémon (Devon Scope Kecleon, Feebas tiles, gifts, one-time encounters)
  - Pokémon that only appear after the National Pokédex, where the data marks them
- **Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, FireRed and LeafGreen** also get:
  - item balls and hidden items, with which part of the map they're in
  - where to Surf, Dive and climb Waterfalls, plus Cut, Rock Smash, Strength and Flash
  - every route, town and cave it connects to
  - a mini map of Hoenn or Kanto and the Sevii Islands, with the route lit up; tap any square to go there
- **Links everywhere:** places under Found in on Poké Data, Where to find it and the best training routes on each Pokémon's data page all open route pages.
- **Smarter training routes:** best routes now rank by real appearance rates.
- Full route details for the other generations will follow in later updates.

## Install
**Online:** open **scooby5328-design.github.io/iV31** (it updates by itself).

**Windows:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now** and it installs through the installer.

**Mac or the Linux .tar.gz:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`
