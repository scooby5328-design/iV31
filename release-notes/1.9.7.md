## What's new
- **Types screen fits without a scroll bar:** on wide windows the weakness and resistance chart shrinks just enough for the chart and the type notes beside it to fit on one screen. On short laptop screens, Gen II and III may still scroll a little because their notes are longer.

## Install
**Online:** open **scooby5328-design.github.io/iV31** (it updates by itself).

**Windows, Mac or the Linux .tar.gz on 1.2.0 or newer:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`

**AppImage:** download the new AppImage below.
