## What's new
- **Main EV instead of numbers:** the Routes tab and each route page now just name the EV that's most common on that route, such as **Speed** or **Sp. Atk**, instead of showing EVs per battle. When two stats are equally common, both are shown.
- Sorting by a stat still ranks routes by how much of it you'll get, and says how you'd get it (walking, surfing, a fishing rod).

## Install
**Online:** open **scooby5328-design.github.io/iV31** (it updates by itself).

**Windows:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now** and it installs through the installer.

**Mac or the Linux .tar.gz:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`

**AppImage:** download the new AppImage below.
