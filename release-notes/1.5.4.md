## What's new
- **Story order is now a toggle that works with everything else.** In EV Training, turn on **Story order** (or click the **Found in** heading) and the table stays in the order you reach places in the game while you:
  - pick the EV you want (HP, Attack, Speed…), which only hides Pokémon that don't give it, or
  - click a stat heading, which now sorts Pokémon found in the same place by that stat.
- Click **Found in** or untick **Story order** to turn it off.

## Install
**Windows, Mac or the Linux .tar.gz on 1.2.0 or newer:** iv31 offers this update by itself. Click **Update now**.

**Linux with apt:** `sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade`

**AppImage:** download the new AppImage below.
