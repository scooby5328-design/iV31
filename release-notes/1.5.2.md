## What's new
- **Found in now follows the game's story order.** Sorting the EV Training table by **Found in** lists Pokémon in the order you reach their areas in the game you picked. In Ruby that's Route 101, Route 103, Route 102, Route 104, Petalburg Woods, Rustboro City and so on, with caves, towns and post-game areas in their place along the way. Click the heading again to reverse it.
- Each Pokémon's list of places is in the same story order, so the first place shown is the earliest one you can reach.
- Covers every main game from Red, Blue and Yellow to Sword and Shield.
