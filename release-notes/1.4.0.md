## What's new
- **Gen I and Gen II.** Red, Blue, Yellow, Gold, Silver and Crystal are now in the game picker. These games work differently, and iv31 follows their rules:
  - **DVs from 0 to 15** instead of IVs, with the HP DV worked out from the other four.
  - **Stat Experience** instead of EVs (enter it under "Stat Exp" if your Pokémon has battled).
  - **No natures.**
  - **One Special stat in Gen I.** In Gen II, Sp. Atk and Sp. Def share the Special DV.
  - **Gen II Hidden Power and shiny check** from the DVs.
  - **Gen I type chart**, with no Dark or Steel and Ghost not affecting Psychic.
  - **Wild locations** for all six games.
- **One game list.** The two dropdowns are now a single list grouped by generation, so every game (including FireRed and LeafGreen) is visible at once.
- **Nature Power.** The Secret Power tab now also shows what Nature Power turns into, from Gen III to Gen VII (for example Stun Spore in tall grass in Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald).
- **Fix:** in Gen III, Secret Power on plain ground (paths and dirt) has no extra effect. It used to say "may paralyze".

## Install
**Already have 1.2.0 or 1.3.1?** iv31 offers this update by itself the next time it starts. Click **Update now**.

**New, or on 1.3.0, 1.1.0 or older?** Download **iv31-Setup-1.4.0.exe.zip** below, right-click it, choose **Extract All**, then run the installer. It installs over older versions and keeps your saved data. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.
