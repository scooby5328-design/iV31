# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone is welcome in the iv31 community, whatever their age, background, experience level, identity or how they play Pokémon. We want this to be a friendly, safe and respectful place for all.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give helpful, constructive feedback and accept it gracefully.
- Focus on what's best for the project and its users.
- Assume good intentions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, harassment, trolling or personal attacks
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Sexual language or imagery
- Sharing anyone's private information without their permission
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing unrelated content
- Any other behaviour that would be inappropriate in a friendly public space

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately using the contact details on their GitHub profile. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may, depending on the situation:

1. Give a private warning
2. Remove or edit comments, commits or issues
3. Temporarily ban someone from the project
4. Permanently ban someone from the project
